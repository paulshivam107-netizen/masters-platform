# Marketing voice (tweets, replies, plans)

Audience: Indian CAT and PI aspirants first. Write for them, not as a builder diary.

## Say
- A specific, checkable claim in the first 8 words, with the topic ("MBA interview", "IIM", "PI") in the first 10.
- Only facts that are true today. Never invent numbers, users or praise. Do not claim users or love until they exist.
- School facts: name the programme (PGP, PGPX, EPGP) and check it against the school's page first. See the school-facts section in the root `CLAUDE.md`.
- Plain words. Do not use the word "pilot". Say "free to use right now".

## Avoid
- Contrast reveals ("It's not X, it's Y"), negation lists, self-answered questions, colon reveals, trailing pile-ons.
- Em dashes, one-line-per-sentence broetry, emoji bullets, engagement bait ("Thoughts?", "Agree?").
- Builder-diary posts ("here's what I shipped"). They read as a diary, not a reason to try it.

## Posting
- Keep the link out of the main tweet. End with "link below 👇" and self-reply with `www.mockmba.com`.
- Post 8-10pm IST. Leave at least about 60 minutes between your own posts.
- Be around for the first 30 minutes and answer every reply.

## Bank hygiene (`marketing/tweets.md`)
- Edit unposted drafts freely. Leave posted records as they were, and mark a draft posted with the date and link once the owner confirms.
- When a draft turns out wrong, fix the bank in the same turn, and tell the owner what was wrong.
